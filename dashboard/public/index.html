How to read this watch

  1. Yesterday: the completed trading session’s close-to-close move.
  2. Today: completed candles show continuation, reversal or uncertainty.
  3. Paper only: a qualifying setup can enter only after it becomes available.

The first complete fifteen-minute candle is available after 08:15 London time, plus collection delay.
